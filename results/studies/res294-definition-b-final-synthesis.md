## Executive synthesis

Definition B is complete as the selected bounded research program: the six-configuration RES-293 study qualified, froze, ran all 30 CPU fits once, sealed the fitted states and prediction bytes before evaluation truth was opened, produced all 21 target-wise exploratory contrasts, and preserved its actual limitations. RES-294 now integrates those results with the frozen G1–G4 evidence, model comparisons, audits, corrections, exclusions, and unresolved questions.

The evidence supports narrow, conditional statements about named procedures and synthetic designs. It does not establish human-athlete validity, causal coaching effects, global latent-state identification, historical cross-version rankings, calibrated superiority, or that nonlinear architecture is necessary. The exact RES-293 point contrasts and descriptive envelopes are in the [study report](res293_representation_learner_information/report.md); all 105 seed-specific contrasts and canonical metrics remain in machine-readable tables.

## Historical genealogy and benchmark evolution

The repository distinguishes historical specimen projections from independently authored public-native research. Eight historical specimens span six shared historical worlds; the latent-capacity-change specimen has a reproducible public specification, while its historical production remains unimplemented publicly. Its historical production design used scrambled Sobol sampling. The separate public-native benchmark uses IID sampling and has distinct DatasetSpec, realization, BenchmarkSpec, and evaluation identities. Shared equations or targets do not make these datasets numerically comparable. See the [benchmark genealogy table](../audits/res284-publication/tables/benchmark-genealogy.csv), the [registry guide](../../docs/benchmarks/index.md), and the [IID evaluation contract](../../docs/benchmarks/iid-latent-capacity-change-public-native-evaluation.md).

Historical G1–G4 names and aliases survive as metadata, but their original rights-cleared protocols, rows, fitted models, or quantitative results were not recovered. The public reports linked above are **new public-native experiments** with their own protocols, realizations, and evaluations. They do not recreate historical G1–G4 and do not fill historical score gaps.

The benchmark program evolved from a synthetic dynamical world, through typed world/data/task/evaluation contracts, to an IID public-native realization, a diverse saved-model panel, and a validity-axis audit suite. The benchmark contract separates semantic benchmark identity, dataset realization, prediction record, and evaluation result. It fixes the prediction origin, visible information, target, support, and metrics; there is no universal scalar leaderboard. Historical capacity-change evaluation and the public-native four-metric evaluation remain separate identities.

The public-native world is a designed synthetic system. Latent capacity `C`, adaptation `A`, suppression `R`, and expressed performance `P` are distinct. Training exposure and future plans are inputs; the observation law maps the system to assessments, prescribed loads, and velocity. The canonical task predicts lift-specific `ΔC` at day 224 from history through day 223 and a declared future plan. This is a forecast under stated synthetic assumptions, not measurement of athlete physiology or a coaching intervention.

### RES-293 execution record

The frozen protocol is `psr:study-protocol:res293-representation-learner-information@1.0.0~333eccbc9029`; the [protocol freeze](../../studies/res293_representation_learner_information/protocol-freeze.json) binds source and receipt hashes, exact canonical data identities, and the one memory-gate engineering amendment. D/E/Q/F all passed. F projected 1.30 GiB RSS against the 12 GiB projection ceiling; the separate 16 GiB process cap was monitored at runtime. In the original uncommitted attempt-5 implementation, `scripts/run_res293.py:148` used the predicate `MemAvailable_bytes >= 16 * 1024**3`; it rejected attempt 5's 17,154,805,760 bytes (about 15.98 GiB) before F. This was an arbitrary free-memory threshold, **not an OOM**. See the [amendment](../../studies/res293_representation_learner_information/qualification/memory-gate-repair-amendment.json), [final qualification receipt](../../studies/res293_representation_learner_information/qualification/pre-fit-qualification.json), and [attempt receipt index](../../studies/res293_representation_learner_information/qualification/preserved-receipts.json).

Six configurations and five paired seeds yielded 30 fits of 60 epochs / 2,340 updates each. They ran on CPU only, used final checkpoints, and completed in 76.61 wall seconds. The peak observed worker RSS was 469,774,336 bytes. The exact five-seed canonical metrics, development diagnostics, fit records, predictions, and hash indices are linked from the [RES-293 report](res293_representation_learner_information/report.md). The shared multiplier envelope is explicitly descriptive; v0.2 withdrew calibrated interval coverage, significance claims, equivalence margins, and material-benefit labels, and none were reintroduced.

## Benchmark-design principles supported by this program

1. **Name the estimand before comparing models.** Expressed performance, latent capacity, state/parameter recovery, observable-record generation, matched response, and future `ΔC` answer different questions.
2. **Bind information and support to the result.** Record forecast origin, visible history, declared future plans, hidden truth, data realization, split, and evaluation keys. A shared display name or target label is not sufficient for numerical comparability.
3. **Treat identification claims separately from prediction.** A predictive fit, local Jacobian rank, or finite search does not prove global recovery or causal equivalence.
4. **Measure data support and label provenance.** A donor-copy bootstrap contains no novel donors; a surrogate pseudo-label is not source truth; a finite panel does not establish population support.
5. **Match the claim to the optimizer and budget.** Equal update counts do not establish equal optimization adequacy, effective capacity, or FLOPs. Report fixed procedures and retain failed or nonconverged fits.
6. **Do not overstate uncertainty.** Small strata, fixed fit panels, outcome-informed selection, Monte Carlo error, and shared dependence each constrain interpretation. An uncalibrated descriptive envelope must not be called a confidence interval.
7. **Preserve negative, null, inconclusive, unsupported, and not-run states.** Absence of evidence is not observed failure or success; evidence for one validity axis does not imply another.
8. **Keep historical reconstruction and new public-native work distinct.** Preserve exact provenance and rights; never convert a public projection or alias into a recovered historical result.

These principles are scoped to the reports and audit suite cited above. They do not imply that all benchmark designs, athlete populations, or future models have been evaluated.

## Unresolved questions and explicit exclusions

- Global identification across the full canonical native support remains unresolved. The tested finite local and multistart results do not settle it.
- The performance frontier beyond the two fixed RES-293 training procedures remains unanswered. No Ridge/closed-form adequacy arm, GRU attribution, larger architecture tournament, or additional hyperparameter search was run.
- RES-293's already-inspected validation panel and five seeds do not provide independent confirmation or uncertainty over new training samples and fit seeds.
- RES-290 did not demonstrate structural non-identification with matched responses. Finite-support and conditional-prediction findings do not resolve source recovery.
- RES-510/511 external human scientific signoff remains `NOT_RUN`; the zero-bias counterfactual refit remains `NOT_RUN`.
- Historical cross-version rank sensitivity remains rejected for lack of matched admissible panels. Real-athlete validity, coaching/clinical causality, and unsupported out-of-distribution claims remain outside evidence.
- Formal archive/DOI/checkpoint release (RES-300/301) and next benchmark design (RES-302/303/304) remain deferred. They were not performed, and no new research issue was created.
