## Architecture and research positioning

The [system model](docs/science/system-model.md) leads from bounded training stimulus to adaptation, capacity, suppression, expressed performance and noisy assessment/load/velocity measurements. Typed contracts independently identify the world, population, intervention, observation process, task, target, data realization, model and evaluation. The strict evaluator aligns prediction keys and rejects duplicate/nonfinite inputs. Public code provides the IID generator, standard comparators, three-seed temporal GRU and point scoring.

The GRU has no physical residual loss or constrained training. Mechanistic priors enter simulation, declared mechanistic comparators and the later inference-time cessation-support restriction. [Research positioning](docs/science/research-positioning.md) connects these to established literature without claiming algorithmic novelty or learned physiology. No trained cross-family model-of-models exists. [Probabilistic methods and claims](docs/studies/probabilistic-capacity-forecasting.md) connect conditional scale H, group calibration G, support truncation T and controlled shifts, retaining both failed point corrections and all principal negative findings.
