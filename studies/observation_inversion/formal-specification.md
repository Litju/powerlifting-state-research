# Observation and identification specification

Authority: the shared world ID and native observation ID in protocol.json; implementations dynamics.py, population.py, observations.py and interventions.py in the latent_capacity_change_with_transient_expression_forecasting package. This study changes neither law nor benchmark identity. It creates an independent experiment, with daily cadence and noise variations explicitly outside the canonical weekly DatasetSpec.

At each day's end x=(A,R), u=(dose,intensity), s=di/(di+Xref). F(x,u)=(alphaA*A+(1-alphaA)*s, alphaR*R+(1-alphaR)*s), alpha=exp(-1/tau). C=B(1+gA*A), P=C(1-gR*R). Original initial states are A[-1]=R[-1]=0; process noise is absent. C and P are kg, states/stimulus/gains dimensionless, tau and cadence days.

For a single observation, a=P(1+epsilonA), L=f*a, v=e+b*(1-L/P)/0.6+epsilonV, f uniform [0.68,0.71), epsilonA normal(0,CV), epsilonV normal(0,sigma m/s). All exponents equal one. The generator rejects invalid assessment/domain observations. Conditional on measured L, noiseless velocity is invertible: r=1-0.6(v-e)/b, P=L/r. At fixed L, dv/dP=bL/(0.6P²), dP/dv=0.6L/(b*r²). Assessment has da/dP=1 at zero noise. Load is part of the velocity channel and itself a bounded noisy assessment proxy; velocity without its load cannot invert P. Since L depends on assessment, channels are not independent unconditional measurements. Conditional noise innovations are independent; the plug-in delta-method combination is an approximate reference, not an optimal posterior.

All channels' observation distributions factor through P. At fixed parameters, dP/d(A,R)=(B*gA*(1-gR*R), -C*gR). Adding channels at one time multiplies this row by channel derivatives and cannot raise latent rank beyond one. A constructive level set takes R2 != R1 and A2=[P/(B*(1-gR*R2))-1]/gA where admissible. Capacity changes along this level set. This proves single-time state/capacity nonuniqueness even with noiseless P. It does not prove whole-history nonobservability.

Structural observability concerns unique initial states from complete ideal outputs under a specified input and known parameters. Local identifiability concerns nearby parameter/state alternatives; global identifiability rules out all alternatives within the stated domain. Practical identifiability concerns finite noisy measurements and conditioning. Predictive reconstructability concerns target error from admissible observations, without requiring unique coordinates. Causal identification is a separate question and is not tested.

With known parameters and known native zero initial state, F deterministically supplies all states: this is privileged forward simulation, not participant inference. For a **free initial state extension**, rest inputs yield A[k]=alphaA^k*A0, R[k]=alphaR^k*R0. Rows of the multi-time observation Jacobian are [B*gA*alphaA^k*(1-gR*alphaR^k*R0), -B*(1+gA*alphaA^k*A0)*gR*alphaR^k]. A nonzero two-time determinant is sufficient for local inversion in this extension at the selected point; it does not establish global identification or transfer to unknown parameters. Degenerate rates, negligible gains or late recovery can impair conditioning.

For native zero initial states, the six independent squat coordinates (baseline, adaptation utilization/retention, suppression utilization/retention, reference stimulus) induce a finite-history P Jacobian. Column scales use normalized coordinates; row units kg. Central differences at an interior midpoint, two steps and all four canonical templates diagnose local sensitivity. Singular values, condition numbers and the relative numerical rank threshold are recorded separately. Full numerical rank suggests local sensitivity at that point only, with discretization evidence; it proves neither a global inverse nor robust parameter recovery under native noise. No numerical aggregate observability score is constructed.

There is an exact global parameter and hidden-state counterexample under constant continue input x=0.6, within dose support but **outside the four historical templates**. Stimulus equals reference stimulus q. Gains are utilization/q; changing q with utilizations and rates fixed changes A and R while preserving gA*A, gR*R, C and P for every time from the native zero start. Observation distributions coincide for equal noise draws. This disproves universal parameter/state identification across all allowed constant-input designs; native-template global identifiability remains unresolved. A small q perturbation under canonical template0 also measures practical near-equivalence, without asserting exact equivalence.

Participants may observe recorded history, past dose/intensity schedules and declared future plan. Their own fitted inverses cannot access hidden coordinates, parameters, true C/P/A/R or future realized observations. Our channel reference estimators consume only observation records and lift name; truth is serialized separately and used only for scoring. Targets P223, C223 and P230 deliberately test distinct claims. The P230 persistence baseline tests prediction only, not canonical DeltaC forecasting. Mean-history baselines test smoothing bias as well as noise reduction. Same sample counts at different cadences have different time spans; no pure cadence causal effect is claimed.
