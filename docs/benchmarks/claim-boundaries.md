# Claim boundaries

Evidence from these records supports claims about specified synthetic tasks and their declared data, input, target, and evaluation contracts.

It does not establish:

- accuracy or validity for real athletes;
- causal effects of training or interventions;
- recovery of biological parameters or latent coordinates;
- athlete responder classification;
- broad out-of-domain generalization;
- qualification or superiority from an isolated checkpoint, score, or historical ranking.

A result should name its benchmark specification, dataset realization, model, evaluation, target support, uncertainty, and known limitations. A stronger claim needs a separate design and evidence that directly supports it.
