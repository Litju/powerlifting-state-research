# Comparability guidance

A shared research area does not make two benchmark scores interchangeable. Before comparing results, align the quantity of interest, prediction origin, visible information, forecast horizon, population construction, intervention support, observation model, data realization, and evaluation aggregation.

The observed-origin and latent-origin capacity-change formulations share one dose-history world and observation family. Their estimands differ: one uses future modeled capacity relative to a noisy observed-origin assessment; the other predicts latent capacity change while retaining the noisy assessment among inputs. Their task and QOI references remain distinct.

The latent-origin task and the latent-capacity task with transient performance expression share a QOI family but use different worlds and observation models. Direct comparison requires matched or stratified support and a common evaluation. The final-origin evaluation is a held-out sample within the class-normalized cross-lift system and does not establish broad real-world or general-domain transfer.

Do not infer improvement, qualification, or scientific superiority from unmatched raw scores. Use a matched re-evaluation with the same target definition, support, information boundary, and evaluation wherever evidence permits.
