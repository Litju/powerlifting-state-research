# Research roadmap

The frozen topology gives later work stable homes without implementing those phases in the initial commit.

- **M3 — reproducible public world:** independently implement and validate one selected synthetic formulation, then publish generated data identities, evaluation, tests, and results.
- **M4 — model evidence:** reconstruct model-family and training-protocol descriptions, add lightweight public comparators, and record checkpoint rights before any weight distribution.
- **M5 — benchmark audits:** RES-277 defines multidimensional validity axes, versioned attack contracts, and evidence-state profiles; RES-278 implements the executable audit harness and version-specific adapters.
- **M6 — research studies:** maintain retrospective study identities separately from benchmarks; add controlled comparisons with changed and held-fixed axes and report negative results.
- **M7 — release hardening:** add integrity and reproducibility automation after the topology is established.
- **M8 — public research release:** prepare citation, documentation, and individually cleared artifacts.
- **M9 — next benchmark:** derive a new scientific formulation from evidence and give it a newly derived scientific name.

The initial registry does not implement benchmark mechanics, regenerate historical data, train models, or publish historical checkpoints.
