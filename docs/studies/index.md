## Findings and limits

**G1:** Assessment, load, and velocity factor through expressed performance. Noiseless expressed-performance recovery does not recover latent capacity. For example, last-observation squat expression error is zero while capacity RMSE is 8.355256 kg in the reported noiseless condition. History averaging can mix changing states; paired noise-channel conclusions are condition-specific. Local Jacobian rank is not a global inverse proof. See the report's mathematical findings and negative-results sections.

**G2:** Compares newest-assessment reconstruction, known-law full bounded MLE, local Laplace Bayesian inference, and a learned Ridge residual. At native noise, only 7 of 256 full-MLE starts converged; 30 of 32 held-out selected fits retained nonconverged finite results. At doubled noise, 22 of 256 starts converged and 23 of 32 selected fits were nonconverged. Those results are scored with explicit status, and bootstrap uncertainty excludes optimization uncertainty. Laplace uncertainty is approximate; its prior sensitivity and calibration do not establish exact Bayesian coverage. Native-template global identification remains inconclusive, despite full local rank at the tested midpoints.

**G3:** Compares finite empirical record resampling, Summary Ridge, and sequence random-feature Ridge. Donor copying supplies no new support. Distribution-discrepancy intervals resample held-out entities **conditional on the fixed generated donor sample**; they do not account for both empirical distributions or guarantee population-level two-sample coverage. The two conditional surrogate families were distinguishable on prediction error, so the proposed observational-equivalence/non-identification demonstration remains inconclusive. Predictive fidelity does not establish causal or structural equivalence.

**G4:** Six arms separate public-only learning, donor bootstrap, surrogate pseudo-labels, fine-tuning, and a compute-matched public-only control, over five fits. There are **9 lift/horizon cells per population and 18 combined per contrast**. The 108 pooled contrast/population/lift/horizon cells are dependent; scientific 95% intervals are per-cell, not family-wise. The executed biases differ from the zero-bias specification. [RES-510](../../studies/corrections/RES-510-v1/interpretation.md) proposes corrected pooled inference, and the [RES-511 v1 initialization erratum](../../studies/corrections/RES-511-v1/interpretation.md) discloses the deviation. Independent scientific approval remains pending. Read the [current correction notice](g4-reader-corrections-v1.md) for original versus corrected counts and distinct conditional interpretations.

**RES-293:** Thirty CPU fits completed across C1–C6 and five paired seeds, with 60 epochs and 2,340 updates per fit. The point differences describe these fixed procedures only. Its shared multiplier envelope is descriptive; sampling coverage was not qualified. It does not establish nonlinear necessity, a strong/optimal linear frontier, information-theoretic necessity, or independent confirmation. The [full report](../../results/studies/res293_representation_learner_information/report.md) retains all 21 contrasts, per-seed rows, metrics, receipts, and limitations.

All studies concern synthetic systems. None demonstrates real-athlete validity, causal training prescriptions, or universal model superiority. Their different targets, data, information privileges, and evaluations do not form a shared numeric leaderboard.
