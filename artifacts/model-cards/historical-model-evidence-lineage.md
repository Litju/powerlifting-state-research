## Evidence inspected

- `artifacts/model-cards/public-native-temporal-capacity-change.md` — the historical reconstruction, preserved identity map, hashes, reported values, and separately labeled canonical replay evidence.
- `models/families.py`, `models/reference-implementations/README.md`, and `models/model-family-specs/README.md` — the registered public model family and the reference/model-spec homes.
- `results/benchmarks/`, `results/studies/`, and `results/manifests/` — surviving result artifacts and study homes. The RES-274 manifest and prediction/result files are PUBLIC_NATIVE IID evidence; they do not establish additional historical models.
- `provenance/PUBLIC_FILE_ORIGINS.csv` and `src/powerlifting_state_research/provenance/historical_sources.py` — file rights and benchmark lineage context, not independent model results.

No historical benchmark, dataset, or evaluation identity is changed here. Historical results on the scrambled-Sobol realization and PUBLIC_NATIVE results on the IID realization remain non-comparable as raw scores because both the DatasetRealization and EVALUATION differ.
